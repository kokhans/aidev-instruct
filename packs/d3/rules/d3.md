# D3

Rules for the latest stable D3 v7 modules.

## Modules

- The project depends on the modules it uses (`d3-scale`, `d3-shape`, `d3-array`, `d3-selection`) and their `@types/d3-*` packages, not on the whole `d3` bundle.
- Pure modules (scales, shapes, arrays, formats, interpolators, layouts) compute values. DOM modules (`d3-selection`, `d3-transition`, `d3-axis`, `d3-zoom`, `d3-brush`, `d3-drag`) touch elements.

## Scales and Shapes

- Scales are typed: `scaleLinear<number, number>()`, `scaleBand<string>()`. Accessors are typed functions of the datum, never `any`.
- Domains come from the data (`extent`, `max`) and are rounded with `.nice()` for axes. Ranges come from the chart size minus margins.
- Data in UTC uses `scaleUtc` and `utcFormat`. Local-time scales only for data recorded in local time.
- Missing values are handled explicitly with `line().defined()` or a filter, never drawn as zero.
- Tick labels use `scale.tickFormat()` or `d3-format`. Numbers and dates in surrounding text use `Intl` formats in the page locale.

## Data Joins

- Selections update with `selection.join()`, with a key function for data that changes order or length.
- Transitions skip or shorten under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Event listeners added with `selection.on("type.name", ...)` are removed with `selection.on("type.name", null)` when the chart is torn down.

## Accessibility

- An SVG chart has `role="img"` and an accessible name, or a `<title>` and `<desc>`. The underlying data is also available as a table or text summary.
- Colour is never the only encoding. Series also differ by label, shape or pattern, and colours pass contrast against the background.
- Colours come from theme tokens or `currentColor`, so charts follow dark mode.

## Sizing and Performance

- Charts scale with a `viewBox` or redraw from a `ResizeObserver` measurement. Never a fixed pixel width.
- More than a few thousand marks render to `<canvas>`, with `d3-quadtree` or `d3-delaunay` for hit testing.
- Force simulations and heavy layouts stop (`simulation.stop()`) when the chart is removed.
